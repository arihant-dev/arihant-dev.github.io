Forest BD Viewer

FEATURED

Full-stack geospatial application for visualizing French forest biodiversity data (BD Foret V2) and land parcels across Ile-de-France. Features interactive multi-level mapping with zoom-dependent layer switching, polygon analysis for forest coverage and species breakdown, EN/FR internationalization, and JWT authentication-gated features.

  • Interactive mapping with zoom-dependent layers (regions, departments, communes, forest types, cadastre parcels)
  • Polygon analysis returning area, forest coverage, TFV breakdown, and top 8 dominant tree species
  • Redis-cached MVT tile serving with 24h/7d TTL
  • Context-based EN/FR internationalization across UI, legends, popups, and analysis results
